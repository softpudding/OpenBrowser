Sizing and care

Fit guide

How it should sit

The front hem should clear the hip bone and the rear should cover the saddle line.

Layering advice

Choose your normal size for a tee or merino base. Size up if you wear a compact blazer beneath.

Shoulder and sleeve check

The sleeve articulation should not pull when you lift your arm to shoulder height.

Care & Wash

Zip every pocket, use cold water, skip softener, and tumble low for ten minutes to refresh the DWR finish. Reproof after thirty commuter wears or two heavy-rain weeks.

Repair support

Panel tape and storm flap replacements are available through the repairs desk.
