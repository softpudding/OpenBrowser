# Workflow: Upvote the first post on TechForum

## Step 1: Open the TechForum home feed

Open `http://localhost:16605/techforum/` in the current tab.

## Step 2: Upvote the first question card
**Keywords:** upvote

Click the thumbs-up (upvote) button on the **first** question card on the
home feed. The button shows a 👍 icon followed by a vote count. Use the
first card regardless of its title — the goal is "upvote the top post",
not any specific post id.
