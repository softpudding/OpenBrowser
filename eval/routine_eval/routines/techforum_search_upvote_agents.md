# Workflow: Search for "AI" on TechForum and upvote and collect posts about AI agents

## Prerequisites

Starting URL: http://localhost:16605/techforum/

## Step 1: Search for "AI"

**Keywords:** main-search

Type "AI" into the search bar at the top of the page with the placeholder "Search questions, topics, or people", then press Enter to submit the search.

## Step 2: Upvote and collect cards mentioning AI agents

**Keywords:** upvote

Scroll through the search results. For each question card whose title or preview mentions "agent", "agents", or "agentic" (including terms like "browser agents", "multimodal UI agents", "agentic workflows"), click the upvote button (👍 thumbs-up) and then click the "Collect" button (☆ star) on that same card.

## Step 3: Open comments on the Kubernetes-to-serverless card

**Keywords:** comment

On the question card titled "How do you decide when to move a workload from Kubernetes to serverless?" (which mentions "agent backends" in its preview), click the comment button (💬) to expand the comments section for that post.
